Our Story

Ghar jaisa khana, dawat jaisi shaan.

Desi Foods started with one simple idea: serve the flavours Karachi grew up with — rich yakhni pulao, desi-ghee karahi and smoky BBQ — without shortcuts. Everything is cooked fresh in our own kitchen at Abdullah Centre, FB Area Block 16.

We're best known for our dumba specialities — the first in the area to introduce a complete Dumba Platter, Dumba Derhya Pulao made in fresh dumba yakhni, and Dumba Chalkki. Whether it's a quick lunch, a family dinner or a dawat for twelve, we've got a platter for it.
