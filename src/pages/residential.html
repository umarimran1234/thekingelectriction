Know the signs

When to call an electrician

Electrical issues rarely fix themselves. If you notice any of these, give us a call before a small problem becomes a dangerous one.

Call {{site.phone}}
  • Breakers that trip again and again
  • Lights that flicker or dim when appliances run
  • Warm, discolored or buzzing outlets and switches
  • A burning smell with no obvious source
  • Aluminum wiring in an older home
  • Not enough outlets — and too many extension cords

FAQ

Homeowner questions, answered

Do you give upfront pricing?

Yes. After we assess the job, you'll get a clear price before any work begins — no surprise charges on the invoice.

Is aluminum wiring really a problem?

Aluminum branch wiring, common in some older homes, can loosen at connections over time and overheat. Pigtailing with approved connectors is a proven way to make those connections safer. We'll inspect and recommend the right fix.

Are your electricians licensed?

Our work is performed by Texas-licensed electricians and backed by a licensed Master Electrician.
